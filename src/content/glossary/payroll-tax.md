---
term: "Payroll tax"
short: "The tax on wages that funds Social Security and Medicare, levied from the first dollar of earnings and capped above a wage threshold."
long: "Payroll tax has no standard deduction and no zero bracket, so it starts where the income tax has not yet begun, and the Social Security half stops at a wage ceiling, so its rate falls as earnings rise past that point. For most households it is the larger of the two federal taxes. Any chart of 'what people pay' that shows income tax alone omits it, and this site says so wherever it does."
source: "Congressional Budget Office, Historical Budget Data, February 2026"
