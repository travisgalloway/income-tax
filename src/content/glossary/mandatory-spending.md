term: "Mandatory spending"
short: "Outlays set by permanent law rather than by an annual appropriation: Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid and the rest of the entitlement programmes."
long: "Mandatory spending flows from eligibility rules written into standing statute, so its level in any year is determined by how many people qualify and what the formula pays them, not by what Congress appropriates that year. Changing it requires changing the law. It is the larger half of the budget and it grows with the population and with medical prices. The figures here are gross, before offsetting receipts."
