---
term: "Mandatory spending"
short: "Outlays set by permanent law rather than annual appropriation: Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid and other entitlement programmes."
long: "Mandatory spending flows from eligibility rules written into standing statute. Its level in any year is set by how many people qualify and what the formula pays. What Congress appropriates does not set it. Changing it requires changing the law. It is the larger half of the budget, and it grows with the population and with medical prices. The figures here are gross, before offsetting receipts."
