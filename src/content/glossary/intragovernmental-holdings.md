short: "Treasury securities held by federal trust funds \u2014 Social Security and Medicare chief among them \u2014 rather than by outside investors."
long: "When a trust fund runs a surplus it is required by law to lend that surplus to the Treasury, which issues it a special security in return. The result is a real legal obligation and, at the same time, one part of the government owing another: $7.74 trillion, 19.4% of the gross debt, as of 7 August 2026. Counting it as debt or netting it out is a choice, which is why the two headline debt measures differ."
