---
term: "Gini index"
short: "A single number from 0 to 1 summarising how unequally income is distributed, where 0 is complete equality and 1 is one recipient taking everything."
long: "The Gini index compresses a whole distribution into one figure, which makes it comparable across years and countries and also means two very different distributions can share a value. The series here is the family Gini, not the household Gini: families exclude people living alone and unrelated household members, so it is a narrower unit and its level is not interchangeable with a household figure."
