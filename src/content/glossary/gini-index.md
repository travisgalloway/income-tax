---
term: "Gini index"
short: "A number from 0 to 1 summarising income inequality, with 0 complete equality and 1 one recipient taking everything."
long: "The Gini index compresses a whole distribution into one figure. That makes it comparable across years and countries. Two very different distributions can therefore share a value. The series here is the family Gini rather than the household Gini. Families exclude people living alone and unrelated household members. The family unit is narrower, and its level is not interchangeable with a household figure."
