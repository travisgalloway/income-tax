short: "The cost of a fixed basket of consumer goods, published monthly; CPI-U is the version covering all urban consumers."
long: "The consumer price index tracks what a fixed basket of goods and services costs over time, so the year-over-year change in it is one measure of inflation. CPI-U is the all-urban-consumers series, the one this site plots. It is published as an index level rather than as a rate, which is why the inflation panel derives the percent change from the index and why the derived series starts one year after the index itself does: the first index year has no prior year to compare against."
