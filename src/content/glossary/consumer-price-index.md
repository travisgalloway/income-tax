short: "The cost of a fixed basket of consumer goods, published monthly; CPI-U is the version covering all urban consumers."
long: "The consumer price index tracks what a fixed basket of goods and services costs over time. The year-over-year change in it is one measure of inflation. CPI-U is the all-urban-consumers series, the one this site plots. The series is published as an index level rather than as a rate. The inflation panel therefore derives the percent change from the index. The derived series starts one year after the index itself. The first index year has no prior year to compare against."
