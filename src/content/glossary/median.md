short: "The middle value of a distribution, with half the cases below and half above, so large values cannot raise it."
long: "Median household income describes the household in the middle. That is why it is used here in place of the mean. A mean rises when income concentrates at the top, even if no middle household gained a dollar. A widening gap between mean and median is itself the finding. Real median household income rose 28.1% over three decades, while the top of the distribution moved much further."
