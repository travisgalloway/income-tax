term: "Median"
short: "The middle value of a distribution: half of the cases fall below it and half above, so a few very large values cannot pull it upward."
long: "Median household income describes the household in the middle, which is why it is used here in place of the mean. A mean rises when income concentrates at the top even if nobody in the middle gained a dollar, so a widening gap between mean and median is itself the story. Real median household income rose 28.1% over three decades while the top of the distribution moved much further."
