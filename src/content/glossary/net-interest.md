---
term: "Net interest"
short: "What the federal government pays to service debt held by the public, less the interest it receives, and the fastest-growing line in the budget."
long: "Net interest is gross interest paid minus interest earned, which mainly nets out the interest credited to federal trust funds. It is not appropriated and cannot be reduced by a vote: it is set by the stock of debt outstanding and by the rates at which that stock is refinanced, so a rate rise feeds through over years as old securities mature and are replaced. FY2015's $223 billion is a recent trough, not the series low."
source: "Congressional Budget Office, Historical Budget Data, February 2026"
