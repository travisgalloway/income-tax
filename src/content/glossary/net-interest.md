---
term: "Net interest"
short: "What the government pays to service debt held by the public, less interest received, and the fastest-growing budget line."
long: "Net interest is gross interest paid minus interest earned, which mainly nets out interest credited to federal trust funds. It is not appropriated and cannot be reduced by a vote. The stock of debt outstanding and the rates that refinance it set the figure. A rate rise feeds through over years as old securities mature and are replaced. FY2015's $223 billion is a recent trough rather than the series low."
