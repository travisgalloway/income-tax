---
term: "Nominal"
short: "Measured in the dollars of the year it happened, with no adjustment for the change in prices since."
long: "A nominal figure is the number as it was recorded. Nominal GDP in FY1995 was $7.56 trillion, and in FY2025 it was $30.5 trillion. Most of that gap is prices rather than output. Nominal is the right basis for anything a dollar was paid on, such as a debt balance. It is the wrong basis for comparing two distant years' worth of goods."
