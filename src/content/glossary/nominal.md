---
term: "Nominal"
short: "Measured in the dollars of the year it happened, with no adjustment for the change in prices since."
long: "A nominal figure is the number as it was recorded. Nominal GDP in FY1995 was $7.56 trillion; nominal GDP in FY2025 was $30.5 trillion, and most of that gap is prices rather than output. Nominal is the right basis for anything a dollar was actually paid on \u2014 a debt balance, an interest coupon, a tax collected \u2014 and the wrong basis for comparing two distant years' worth of goods."
