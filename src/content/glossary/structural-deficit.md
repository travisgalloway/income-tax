---
term: "Structural deficit"
short: "The part of the deficit that would remain if the economy were running at its full potential, so it does not close on its own when growth returns."
long: "Splitting a deficit into a structural and a cyclical part asks how much of it is the business cycle and how much is the permanent gap between what the government has committed to spend and what its tax code raises. Only the structural part requires a policy change to close. The split depends on an estimate of potential output, which is not observed and is revised, so the two components are model output rather than measurement."
