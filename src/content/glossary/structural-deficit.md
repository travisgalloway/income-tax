---
term: "Structural deficit"
short: "The part of the deficit that would remain at full potential output, so it does not close when growth returns."
long: "Splitting a deficit into a structural and a cyclical part asks how much of it is the business cycle. The rest is the permanent gap between committed spending and what the tax code raises. Only the structural part requires a policy change to close. The split depends on an estimate of potential output, which is not observed and is revised. The two components are model output rather than measurement."
