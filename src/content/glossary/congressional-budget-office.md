long: "CBO scores legislation and publishes two historical files this site reads directly. Historical Budget Data supplies outlays, revenue and the deficit. Historical Economic Data supplies GDP, prices and rates. Both extend past the last actual fiscal year as a baseline projection. A baseline projection forecasts current law rather than predicting what Congress will do. Every chart here draws the projected years as a separate dashed branch past a marked boundary. The two are never read as one series."
