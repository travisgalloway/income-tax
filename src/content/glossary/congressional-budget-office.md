long: "CBO scores legislation and publishes two historical files this site reads directly: Historical Budget Data, which is where outlays, revenue and the deficit come from, and Historical Economic Data, which is where GDP, prices and rates come from. Both extend past the last actual fiscal year as a baseline projection, which is a forecast of current law rather than a prediction of what Congress will do, and every chart here draws the projected years as a separate dashed branch past a marked boundary so the two are never read as one series."
