short: "The federal tax collector, whose Statistics of Income tables source every figure here about who pays income tax."
long: "The IRS publishes Statistics of Income, tabulations drawn from filed returns rather than from a survey. The shares on the households route are therefore counts of what was reported rather than estimates of what was earned. Two consequences follow, and both are stated where they matter. The tables are organised by tax year rather than the fiscal year the rest of this site uses. Refundable credits are excluded from the series, which overstates the effective rate at the bottom of the distribution."
