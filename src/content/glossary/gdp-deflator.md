---
term: "GDP deflator"
short: "The price index converting nominal GDP into real GDP, covering all output rather than one household basket."
long: "The GDP deflator covers all domestic output, including investment goods, government purchases and exports as well as consumer goods. The deflator is therefore broader than the consumer price index, and the two move differently. That difference is why constant-dollar series here do not all share a base. Real GDP is deflated to FY2025 chained dollars. Household income and bracket thresholds are deflated by CPI-U to 2024 dollars."
