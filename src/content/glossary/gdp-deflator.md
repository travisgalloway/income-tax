---
term: "GDP deflator"
short: "The price index used to convert nominal GDP into real GDP, covering everything the economy produces rather than one household basket."
long: "Because it covers all domestic output \u2014 investment goods, government purchases and exports as well as consumer goods \u2014 the GDP deflator is a broader measure than the consumer price index, and the two move differently. That difference is why constant-dollar series on this site do not all share a base: real GDP is deflated to FY2025 chained dollars, while household income and bracket thresholds are deflated by CPI-U to 2024 dollars."
source: "Congressional Budget Office, Historical Economic Data, February 2026"
