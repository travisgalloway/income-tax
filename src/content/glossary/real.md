term: "Real"
short: "Measured in the dollars of one fixed year, so two distant years compare without prices standing in for growth."
long: "A real series divides a nominal one by a price index and restates every year in the same dollars. Real GDP rose 9.95 times between fiscal 1950 and fiscal 2025. Nominal GDP rose far more, and the difference is inflation rather than output. Every real figure has to name its base year, because 'real' alone says nothing. FY2025 chained dollars and 2024 dollars are different units, and their series do not line up."
