---
term: "Real"
short: "Measured in the dollars of one fixed year, so that two distant years can be compared without the change in prices standing in for growth."
long: "A real series divides a nominal one by a price index and restates every year in the same dollars. Real GDP rose 9.95 times between fiscal 1950 and fiscal 2025; nominal GDP rose far more, and the difference is inflation, not output. Every real figure has to name its base year, because 'real' on its own says nothing: FY2025 chained dollars and 2024 dollars are different units and their series do not line up."
