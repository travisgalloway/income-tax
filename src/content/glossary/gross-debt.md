term: "Gross debt"
short: "The total federal debt outstanding: debt held by the public plus intragovernmental holdings, and the number usually reported as 'the national debt'."
long: "Gross debt is the largest of the debt measures and the one that reaches the headlines, but it double-counts in an economic sense, because the intragovernmental part is the government's claim on itself. Debt held by the public is the measure to use when the question is what the government has borrowed from the economy. Both are shown here, and a chart that gives only one should say which."
