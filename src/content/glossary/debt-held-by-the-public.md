term: "Debt held by the public"
short: "The portion of federal debt owed to investors outside the federal government: households, banks, pension funds, foreign holders and the Federal Reserve."
long: "This is the measure economists use, because it is the part of the debt that was financed by borrowing from someone and that has to be refinanced in a market. It stood at $32.14 trillion, 80.6% of the gross total, on 7 August 2026. It excludes the Treasury securities that federal trust funds hold, which are an internal claim of the government on itself."
