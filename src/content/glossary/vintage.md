---
term: "Vintage"
short: "The publication date of the data behind a figure, meaning which release of a revised series it came from."
long: "Official statistics are revised. The same fiscal year's GDP has several published values as estimates are refined. Two charts drawn from different releases of one series will disagree without either being wrong. Every figure on this site carries its vintage next to its source. A number here can then be reconciled against the release it came from rather than against whatever is current today."
