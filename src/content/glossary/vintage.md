---
term: "Vintage"
short: "The publication date of the data behind a figure \u2014 which release of a revised series it came from, not the years the series covers."
long: "Official statistics are revised: the same fiscal year's GDP has several published values as estimates are refined, and two charts drawn from different releases of the same series will disagree without either being wrong. Every figure on this site carries its vintage next to its source for that reason, so a number here can be reconciled against the release it came from rather than against whatever is current today."
source: "Congressional Budget Office, Historical Economic Data, February 2026"
