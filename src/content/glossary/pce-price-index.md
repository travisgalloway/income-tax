short: "A price index built from what households actually spend rather than a fixed basket; core PCE excludes food and energy."
long: "The personal consumption expenditures price index reweights itself as households substitute between goods, so it usually reads a little lower than the consumer price index over the same span. Core PCE strips out food and energy, the two components that move most on supply shocks, to show the slower-moving part of inflation. Like CPI-U it is published as an index level, so the rate this site plots is a year-over-year change derived from the index."
