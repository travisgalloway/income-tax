term: "Roll-call vote"
short: "A recorded vote entering each member's yea or nay individually, so a per-party split can be counted rather than inferred."
long: "A chamber may also pass a bill by voice vote or unanimous consent, and no roll call then exists. The House passed the CARES Act by voice on 27 March 2020. One chamber of one law here therefore has no split. That absence is rendered as missing data, never as unanimity and never as 0-0. A voice vote records that no member demanded a count, not that no member objected."
