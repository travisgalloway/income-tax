term: "Roll-call vote"
short: "A recorded vote in which each member's yea or nay is entered individually, so a per-party split can be counted rather than inferred."
long: "A chamber may also pass a bill by voice vote or unanimous consent, and no roll call then exists \u2014 the House passed the CARES Act by voice on 27 March 2020, so one chamber of one law here has no split at all. That absence is rendered as missing data, never as unanimity and never as 0-0, because a voice vote records that nobody demanded a count, not that nobody objected."
