---
term: "Chained dollars"
short: "A constant-dollar measure built from a price index whose weights update every year rather than at one base year."
long: "A fixed-weight deflator holds the basket of goods constant. That overstates growth the further a year sits from the base year, because consumers substitute away from whatever became expensive. A chained index re-weights annually and links the results, so it tracks a moving basket. The cost is that chained-dollar components no longer add up to their total exactly. Real GDP here is in FY2025 chained dollars."
