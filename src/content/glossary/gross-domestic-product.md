term: "Gross domestic product"
abbr:
  - "GDP"
short: "The value of everything the country produces in a year, and the denominator most federal figures here use."
long: "GDP is the total market value of final goods and services produced within the United States over a period. It is the denominator that makes a federal figure comparable across decades. $40 trillion of debt means one thing against a $23 trillion economy and another against a $7 trillion one. That is why every debt, outlay and revenue chart here offers a share-of-GDP reading beside the dollar one. Measured against GDP a series can fall while its dollar figure rises, and both readings are true."
