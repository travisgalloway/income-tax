---
term: "Fiscal year"
short: "The federal government's accounting year, running from 1 October to 30 September and named for the calendar year it ends in."
long: "Fiscal 2025 began on 1 October 2024 and ended on 30 September 2025. Every budget, debt and revenue figure on this site is on the fiscal-year basis unless it is explicitly labelled a calendar year, which matters because the two disagree by a quarter: household income and price series here are calendar-year, federal outlays and receipts are not. A figure that mixes the two without saying so is comparing nine months against twelve."
source: "Congressional Budget Office, Historical Budget Data, February 2026"
