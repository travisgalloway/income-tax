---
term: "Incidence"
short: "Who actually bears the cost of a tax, which need not be whoever is legally required to remit it."
long: "The employer's half of the payroll tax is remitted by the employer and is widely held to be borne by the worker in the form of lower wages; a corporate tax is remitted by a company and falls somewhere among its shareholders, workers and customers. Statutory liability is a fact about the law and incidence is an economic judgement, so any figure that assigns a tax to a group has made an assumption that ought to be stated."
