---
term: "Discretionary spending"
short: "Outlays that Congress sets each year through the appropriations process, covering defence, most agencies, and non-entitlement programmes."
long: "Discretionary spending is the part of the budget that an annual appropriations bill actually decides. It is roughly a quarter of outlays, split between defence and everything else. The same category is what lapses in a shutdown. Mandatory spending and net interest are both set elsewhere. An argument about 'cutting spending' confined to appropriations is therefore arguing over a shrinking share of the total."
