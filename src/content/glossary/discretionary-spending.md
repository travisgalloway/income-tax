---
term: "Discretionary spending"
short: "Outlays that Congress sets each year through the appropriations process, covering defence, most agencies, and non-entitlement programmes."
long: "Discretionary spending is the part of the budget that an annual appropriations bill actually decides. It is roughly a quarter of outlays, split between defence and everything else, and it is the part that lapses in a shutdown. Because mandatory spending and net interest are both set elsewhere, arguments about 'cutting spending' that are confined to appropriations are arguing over a shrinking share of the total."
source: "Congressional Budget Office, Historical Budget Data, February 2026"
